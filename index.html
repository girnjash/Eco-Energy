01 / THE CHALLENGE

Our waste problem
is also an opportunity.

Growing cities produce enormous amounts of waste. When it is dumped in landfills or burned without proper controls, it can pollute air, soil and water, while releasing greenhouse gases.

Waste-to-energy offers another option: recover useful energy from suitable waste that cannot be prevented, reused or recycled.
